I'm Gabriele, a Data Science and AI researcher from Trieste, Italy.

What I enjoy most is learning — not just accumulating facts, but building a coherent picture of how things work. I'm drawn to research that sits at the intersection of ideas: where mathematics meets computation, where theory meets practice, where one field's tools unexpectedly solve another field's problems.

Right now I'm mostly thinking about self-supervised learning, planning, and how machines can build useful representations of the world without being told exactly what to look for.
